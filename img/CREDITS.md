## A note on what these pictures show

Kyrgyzstan's public landmarks — squares, parks, museums, the circus, the
memorials, the lakes — are well covered by free-licensed photography, and
every one of those slots holds a photograph of the actual place.

Most of the restaurants, cafes, hostels and shopping malls in this app are
private businesses, and no free-licensed photograph of those premises
exists. Those slots hold a **representative** image instead: the dish the
place is known for, or the kind of room you would be booking. Every one of
them is listed in `REPRESENTATIVE` in `photos.js`, and the app marks them
on screen — a badge on the picture, and a line of explanation on the place
screen — so a representative photo is never mistaken for the venue.

The entries below are marked accordingly.
